In Their Own Words

Real Women.
Real Transformation.

These are not before-and-after stories. These are the accounts of women who chose to return to themselves — and changed everything in the process.

Agni Hotra ceremony, Bali Retreat 2024
Case Study 01

Kateryna
Vienna → Bali

@katerinfedorova
Vienna, Austria → Ubud, Bali · 2 years of work together
"It is because of Ana that I stepped through a door into this life. And I just — walked through it. Wide open."
Before

Recovering from burnout. A stable career that felt empty. A long-term relationship that had become a comfort zone. Feeling successful on the outside — and hollow on the inside.

After

Left her corporate career. Ended a 12-year relationship. Moved to Bali. Now building a business in China while living between Bali and Asia — fully on her own terms.

Katya came to her first session curious about nail-walking. She had never tried any spiritual or somatic practice. Within minutes on the nails, she felt something unlock — energy channels she hadn't known were blocked.

Over two years of retreats, circles, and deep somatic work, Katya developed what she calls "the skill of trusting her body." That skill — feeling a vague discomfort and following it — led her to Bali, to a new life, and to herself.

"It is because of Ana that I stepped through a door into this life. With each practice, something new unlocked. And I learned that trusting your body — feeling a vague discomfort and following it — is the most important skill you can develop."

— Kateryna, Bali 2024

Case Study 02

Viktoriia
The Body Remembers

@vikunchikk
Vienna, Austria · 1.5 years of work together
"Before, I couldn't even imagine how emotions live in the body. Now I feel it clearly — and I can no longer pretend I don't notice."
Before

Eating disorder. Chronic stress. Working only for money, not meaning. A pattern of stress-eating she couldn't stop — no matter how hard she tried.

After

Stopped stress-eating — without willpower, through somatic work. Found work that genuinely lights her up. Learned to say no without guilt, and to receive without shame.

Viktoria came drawn to the complexity of Ana's approach — both psyche and body together. She knew that working with only one dimension was never going to be enough.

The turning point came quietly: she noticed she had simply stopped stress-eating. No decision. No discipline. The pattern dissolved — because the need underneath it had been met, through the body.

"I call it the method of tender therapy. Without aggression. Without forcing anything. Because when you're looking at a difficult truth about yourself — pressure closes you down. But softness — softness lets you open."

— Viktoriia, Vienna
Mauritius · after the Bali Retreat
Case Study 03

Dariia
The Tenth Door

@dariia.av
Mauritius · Individual mentorship
"The things that used to work — stopped working. Regular therapy wasn't landing for me. And then I found the only path that actually led back to myself."
Before

At a threshold — nothing that once worked was effective anymore. Tried different types of therapy. Felt stuck with no clear direction forward.

After

Found deep contact with herself through breathwork. Understood that the right guide makes all the difference — someone who feels you, not just follows a method.

Darya had been skeptical of this kind of work. She had tried many things — and was convinced that meditation and somatic practice simply wouldn't work on her.

What changed her mind was Ana's ability to feel each person individually — to know where to guide, without a fixed script. "If you have ten doors and you've tried nine — don't refuse the tenth," she says now.

"Ana feels every person. She invests enormously — emotionally — into every situation. There is no one path for everyone. And she knows how to find yours."

— Dariia, Mauritius
